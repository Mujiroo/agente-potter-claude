Three kinds of puzzles, one calm afternoon.

Calm & Cozy Puzzles brings together gentle word searches, easy sudoku and simple mazes, printed large and clear on roomy 8.5 x 11 inch pages. No timers, no pressure: just a quiet moment with a pencil and a warm cup of something.

What's inside

40 large print word searches on cozy themes, from Cozy Mornings and Rainy Afternoon to Picnic Day and Gentle Sleep. Words read forward only, never backwards.
20 easy sudoku, each with one single solution.
10 mazes with thick, easy-to-follow lines, from shorter to longer paths.
How to Play and Helpful Tips pages to get started.
Full solutions at the back of the book.

A thoughtful, screen-free gift for parents, grandparents and anyone who loves to slow down and unwind.
